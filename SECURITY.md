# Security and privacy reports

Please report suspected security or privacy issues through GitHub private
vulnerability reporting. Do not open a public issue containing bill contents,
personal or health information, credentials, payment details, or exploit
details.

If private vulnerability reporting is unavailable, use the contact method on
https://medicalbillreader.com/contact and include only the minimum information
needed to arrange a secure follow-up. Never attach a real medical bill.

## Scope priorities

- exposure, logging, caching, or unintended retention of uploaded documents
- transmission of bill contents or analysis results to analytics or advertising
- upload validation, rate-limit, entitlement, checkout, or webhook bypasses
- authentication, injection, dependency, or deployment-secret vulnerabilities

Please do not submit real personal information, health information, credentials,
payment data, or destructive proof-of-concept material when reporting a problem.
